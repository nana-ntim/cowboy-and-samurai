Team Cowboy

The Tale of Blade and Gun

In 1961 Akira Kurosawa made Yojimbo: a lone samurai walks into a town split between two gangs and plays them against each other.

Three years later Sergio Leone remade it as A Fistful of Dollars, and the samurai became a cowboy. Same stranger, same code, different hat. We put them both on a bun, parked them in Accra, and left the ending to you.

Yaa Asantewaatakes on the Cowboy and Samurai.

The newest fighter on the menu is one of ours.

In 1900, Yaa Asantewaa, Queen Mother of Ejisu, led the Asante war against British rule to protect the Golden Stool. When a cowboy and a samurai rolled into Accra, she was the obvious answer. Her burger is built from home.
